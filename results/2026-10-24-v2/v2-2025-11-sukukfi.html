Incomplete validation in SafeTokenTransfers.safeTransferFrom allows asset inflation

Model confidence: 97%

File: SafeTokenTransfers.sol

Function: safeTransferFrom()

Category: SOLVENCY

The `safeTransferFrom` helper only verifies that the recipient’s balance increased by the expected amount. It does **not** check that the sender’s balance decreased accordingly. A malicious ERC‑20 token could implement `transferFrom` that credits the recipient without debiting the sender (or credits a different amount), causing the vault to record a deposit that was never actually funded.

Attack path: 1. Attacker deploys a malicious ERC‑20 token where `transferFrom` simply mints `amount` to the `recipient` and returns `true` without reducing the `sender` balance.

Impact: The vault can be forced to mint shares backed by non‑existent assets, leading to loss of all pooled funds and complete insolvency.

Recommendation: After performing the transfer, also verify that the sender’s balance decreased by the expected amount (or use `IERC20Metadata(token).allowance` checks and rely on OpenZeppelin’s `safeTransferFrom` without additional balance checks). Alternatively, reject tokens that do not conform to the ERC‑20 standard via a whitelist or by using a pull‑payment pattern that checks both sides.
