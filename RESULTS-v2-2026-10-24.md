# Results of the pre-registered measurement of version 2

Measured on 24 October 2026 between 06:08 and 06:57 UTC, after the pre-registration in [PREREGISTRATION-v2-2026-10-24.md](PREREGISTRATION-v2-2026-10-24.md) was merged at 06:08:20 UTC. The first measurement is in [RESULTS-2026-10-24.md](RESULTS-2026-10-24.md).

## Result in one paragraph

Version 2 found none of the 40 known High and Medium findings: 0 of 31 on the four contests of the first measurement (set A, tuned on) and 0 of 9 on the three new contests (set B). It reported 31 hypotheses, none of which has the same root cause as a known finding. It read 29 percent of the in-scope Solidity by size (8 to 100 percent per contest). 15 of the 40 known findings sit in a file that was read; none of those 15 was found. The defects of the first run are gone: all seven jobs ended with a report, the repository with submodules (2026-01-olas) was scanned, and every report states how much of the code was read.

## Decision rule

The pre-registration said: with no hit at all we publish the result next to the first one, we describe the paid scan only as a generator of hypotheses to review by hand, and we put the question of the price and of the offer to the owner. The sentence in the rule assumed that most of the code would be read. It was not (29 percent by size), so the numbers above are the ones to quote: 0 of 40 known findings, with 8 to 100 percent of the in-scope code read. The README now describes the paid scan as a generator of hypotheses. The question of the price and the offer is with the owner.

## What the measurement shows, without having tuned anything on it

- 25 of the 40 known findings sit in files that were never read. The cap of six parts and the ranking decided that: 19 of the 23 in olas (only 3 of its 102 files were read, 88 KB of 1.1 MB), 3 of the 4 in ekubo (the Oracle and TWAMM files), 2 of the 4 in sukukfi (the Vault and the share token) and 1 of the 2 in intuition.
- Of the 15 known findings whose main file was read, none was found. Reading the right file was not enough for these models.
- The weakest source produced most of the noise. In olas the free Nemotron model read all six parts and reported nothing. Because the result was empty, the rule for a second attempt ran the two highest-ranked parts again through another strong source, Mistral codestral, and that produced all 13 raw hypotheses (12 after removing duplicates), all in Treasury.sol, mostly "missing access control" or "missing validation" statements. We did not check them against the code, and none of the 23 known Olas findings concerns that file. In sukukfi, 6 of the 7 hypotheses came from the same Mistral model on the first part and read like a checklist (no pause mechanism, no slippage protection, no minimum deposit). So the second-attempt rule, which was meant to avoid an empty report, mostly turned an empty answer from one model into a list from a weaker one.
- The scan runs on one free model source in practice. Over the seven runs Cerebras answered 402 (payment required) every time it was asked (51 times), Together answered 402 (36), SambaNova answered 401, an invalid key (36), Groq refused 16 requests as too large and 15 as rate limited and returned an empty answer 5 times. OpenRouter with the free Nemotron model answered most parts, at about one to two minutes a part. Mistral answered three parts. A paid scan that depends on this is as reliable as these free tiers.
- Where a known finding sits in a read file and the hypothesis is in the same file, the root cause still differed. The tables below show the same-file cases.

## A defect found during the runs

When a model leaves the FUNCTION line empty, the parser read the following line (the description) into the function field. In one report (ekubo) the Function line therefore shows the start of the description. The same defect can make the filter for invented functions drop a hypothesis that has no function, because the "function" it looks for is a piece of description. The two hypotheses dropped in sukukfi ("Insufficient Validation of Investment Vault", "Missing Event Emission for Critical State Changes") are contract-wide and probably fell for that reason, not because the model named a function that does not exist. We did not store the raw model text, so we cannot be sure. The old runner had the same parser. A fix with unit tests was not applied during the measurement, so that the measured pipeline stays the frozen one. It was deployed after the publication of this page as version 2.1 and has not been measured: [pipeline/v2.1](pipeline/v2.1).

## Correction of 24 October 2026, found after publication: the measured prompts lacked the pattern block

The runner builds one block of its prompt from a folder of historical findings (knowledge/3ilm, the corpus of past contest findings). The server that handles paid scans has that folder. The folder in which we ran this measurement did not, so the prompts of the seven runs did not contain the block of historical patterns with accepted and rejected examples that production prompts do contain. The statement above and in the pre-registration that the only difference between the measured copy and production is the delivery step was therefore wrong: this was a second difference.

The numbers on this page are what they are, measured without that block. We do not know whether the block changes the result. The benchmark of version 3 measures both ways, with and without the block, on contests chosen by a rule fixed beforehand. See [PREREGISTRATION-v3-2026-10-24.md](PREREGISTRATION-v3-2026-10-24.md).

## Limits

Seven small contests and 40 known findings. We did the matching and we are an interested party, which is why the full tables are here. The same repository can give another answer on another day because the model sources are free tiers and not deterministic. Each contest was run once. A model may have seen material about these contests. This is our own measurement, not an independent one. Set A was tuned on, so its zero says less than the zero on set B.

## What happens next

The README describes the paid scan as a generator of hypotheses. Contests whose reports are published after today are set C and will be measured with the same method. Changes to the pipeline, such as another way of ranking files or more parts, are a new version with its own pre-registration. The model sources are the first thing to look at: three of the six are dead today.
