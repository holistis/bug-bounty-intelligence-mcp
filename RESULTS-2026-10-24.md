# Results of the pre-registered baseline test (24 October 2026)

This is the outcome of the test described in [PREREGISTRATION-2026-10-24.md](PREREGISTRATION-2026-10-24.md). Nothing in the set, the scoring rule or the prediction was changed after the run.

## Short version

The paid scan, as it runs today, found 0 of the 31 High and Medium findings in the four contests. It reported 2 hypotheses in total, and neither matches a finding in the official reports. One job failed and two returned empty reports. Our prediction was "at most 2 of 31", so the prediction held, and the decision rule applies: we do not describe the paid scan as something that finds bugs on projects it has not seen.

## What this shows, and what it does not

- It shows that on four small contests the scan found none of 31 findings. It reached 3 to 7 percent of the source, read test and mock files as if they were scope, returned empty reports twice and failed once.
- It does not show how well a version without these limits would do. These four contests are now used up as a test of this version. A changed version needs a new pre-registration and contests it was not tuned on.
- The matching is ours. We are an interested party, which is why the full tables are public.
- Two things that affect a paying customer, found on the way: a job on a repository with submodules fails without a report, and in the production code path an empty report is delivered as a finished job (the log only warns). Both need a decision before the paid route is promoted.

## What changes

- The README states the limits and links this page.
- The paid scan is not described as something that finds bugs on unseen projects, and it is not listed as such anywhere we control.
- Any improvement (skipping test and mock folders, reading the whole scope in chunks, handling submodules) will be pre-registered first and measured on contests not used here.
